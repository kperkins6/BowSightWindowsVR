# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-26

- Regenerated both handed sets, including Long Bow, with approximately 40% wider remaining sections and 15% shorter openings.
- Retained the existing arrow-shelf height.
- Kept 34 mesh paths per hand and mutually exclusive FOMOD choices.
- Preserved source models, original weight tables/UVs and skeleton/material/collision blocks.
- Published portable source/tools and Nexus preparation documents; mesh archives remain local pending upstream permissions.

- Opened the notch-side portions of ornate bows and Bound Bow effect surfaces while preserving the far side.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26
