## Permission record to complete

For each source family, record the original author, original resource URL, relevant mesh paths, date, exact permission scope, attribution requirements, redistribution restrictions and where the evidence is retained. Do not publish private correspondence unless the correspondent agrees.

Once permissions are established, a bundled FOMOD can be attached to a future release and prepared for Nexus. Until then, the public release is the original code/documentation/toolkit. A locally generated FOMOD remains a local build artifact.
