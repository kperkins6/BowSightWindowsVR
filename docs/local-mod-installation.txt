Bow Sight Windows 0.3.0 - current MGO meshes

Install this ZIP through Mod Organizer 2. The FOMOD installer offers:
  Left-handed: bow in RIGHT hand, string drawn with LEFT hand.
  Right-handed: bow in LEFT hand, string drawn with RIGHT hand.
Choose exactly one. Reinstall and replace the mod to change handedness.
Disable the older [NoDelete] Long Bow Left-Hand Sight Window entry.
Place this package after the installed bow-mesh providers so its meshes win.

Includes standard playable Skyrim/Dawnguard/Dragonborn bow designs, Bound Bow,
Drainspell Bow and existing unique-model variants used by this setup. There
are 34 mesh paths per handedness, including first-person and display variants.
Every mesh, including Long Bow, is regenerated from the untouched source.
Compared with 0.2.0, the remaining section is approximately 40% wider and the
opening is 15% shorter. The arrow shelf stays at its existing height.
No crossbows or NPC-only dummy weapons are included.

Only visual meshes are installed. Existing skeletons, grip/arrow placement,
collision, animation references, shaders/materials and Bow Inertia settings
are retained. Glass and other ornate bows have the notch-side portion narrowed
while the far side is retained. Bound Bow flame/refraction meshes also have
an opening. New edge vertices on these effect sheets use the hardware limit
of four bone influences; original vertices retain their original weights.

These files are derived from your currently installed MGO/Cathedral/PGPatcher
and unique-bow providers, not a texture-independent vanilla distribution.
Keep the current texture/material/unique-bow dependencies. Mesh replacements
from later mods or a rerun of PGPatcher can overwrite the cutouts.

Geometry, weights, bounds, preservation and both FOMOD choices were checked
offline. The user approved the 0.2.0 appearance; these revised proportions
await headset evaluation. Disable this separate mod to restore your previous
models. Previous 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 archives are preserved for rollback.
